About us

Who We Are

Runya Ventures Private Limited is a newly incorporated private limited company based in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, specializing in furniture manufacturing, trading, and supply.

Founded with a commitment to excellence, Runya Ventures serves government institutions, schools, offices, hospitals, and businesses throughout India. Our team combines traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing techniques to deliver superior quality products.
